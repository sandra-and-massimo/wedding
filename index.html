Getting there

By air

Ancona-Falconara
The nearest airport: about 75 km, under an hour by car.
Pescara
About 100 km, a little over an hour by car.
Bologna
About 3 hours by train or by car.
Rome
About 3 hours by car, about 5 by train.

By train

Porto San Giorgio-Fermo station is on the Adriatic line, with direct trains from Bologna and Milan. From Ancona it takes under an hour.

By car

A14 motorway, exit Fermo-Porto San Giorgio.

We recommend renting a car: public transport between the towns is limited.
